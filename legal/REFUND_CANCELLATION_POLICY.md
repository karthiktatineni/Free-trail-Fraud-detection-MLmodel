# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026  
**Application:** Fraud Engine Commercial Subscriptions & Paid Usage Tiers

---

## 1. Free Developer Tier & Evaluation

Fraud Engine offers a free developer tier allowing developers to test our risk evaluation algorithms, generate up to 3 test API keys, and evaluate performance before committing to a commercial plan.

We strongly encourage prospective customers to test our sandbox and developer playground to confirm technical compatibility prior to purchasing a paid tier.

---

## 2. Commercial Subscriptions & Billing

- **Billing Cadence:** Paid tiers are billed in advance on a recurring monthly or annual basis.
- **Payment Processing:** Charges are processed securely through our authorized payment processing provider.
- **Upgrades & Downgrades:** Plan upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect at the conclusion of the active prepaid billing cycle.

---

## 3. Subscription Cancellation Process

You can cancel your subscription at any time:
1. **Self-Service:** Navigate to Developer Dashboard &gt; Account &gt; Billing &gt; **Cancel Plan**.
2. **Email Support:** Submit a cancellation request with your registered organization email to `billing@fraudengine.io`.

### Terms Upon Cancellation:
- Your paid plan remains active until the end of your current prepaid billing term.
- No further automated charges will be applied.
- At the end of the term, your account reverts to the Free Developer Tier.
- Your existing customer records and API keys remain accessible subject to standard free tier quotas.

---

## 4. Refund Policy

### A. 14-Day Refund Window (First-Time Commercial Purchases)
If you purchase a paid commercial plan for the first time and find it does not meet your technical requirements, you may request a **full refund within fourteen (14) calendar days** of your initial transaction. To initiate a refund, email `billing@fraudengine.io` with your account details.

### B. Monthly Subscription Renewals
Subsequent monthly renewals are generally non-refundable once the billing cycle has started, as dedicated compute capacity is provisioned. If you experience verified service downtime exceeding our SLA targets, you may request service credits or prorated billing adjustments.

### C. Annual Subscription Plans
If you cancel an annual plan after the initial 14-day window, you are eligible for a prorated refund covering the remaining unused full months, calculated based on standard monthly pricing.

---

## 5. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be granted where:
- An account has been suspended for deliberate violations of our [Acceptable Use Policy](file:///legal/ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) (such as API abuse, model scraping, or credential attacks).
- Overage usage charges reflect API requests that were successfully processed within agreed rate limits.

---

## 6. Account Closure & Data Purge

If you request permanent account closure under our [Privacy Policy](file:///legal/PRIVACY_POLICY.md):
- Active API keys are immediately revoked.
- Stored customer evaluation records are deleted from primary systems within **30 business days**.

---

## 7. Billing Contact

For refund requests, invoice inquiries, or billing support:
- **Email:** `billing@fraudengine.io`
- **Response SLA:** Within 1–2 business days
